# ScanBack Kids — A4 Kiss-cut Printer Shortlist and RFQ (research only)

**Reviewed:** 21 October 2026. **Status:** candidate vendors only; none contacted, contracted, ordered, tested or approved. No printer is certified to manufacture ScanBack Kids stickers yet.

## Critical business requirement
For **each paid ScanBack customer**, print a **unique** full A4 backing sheet with six different original 3D artworks/poses (as chosen by customer), changing child's name, changing item text, and SIX DISTINCT permanent QR URLs. This is **variable-data, print-on-demand** per A4 sheet, not 25 identical sticker sheets. The release liner must remain intact (kiss-cut only). A4 stays flat and is packaged in a rigid mailer. For a 6-pack prototype use a 2-column x 3-row centered layout.

This per-order personalization is an absolute sourcing gate. Standard sticker-sheet offerings, even when A4 and waterproof, may be **unsuitable if they require one master design repeated across an MOQ**.

## Exact technical enquiry — copy into vendor chat/email ONLY when the owner chooses to do so

> Hello, I am developing ScanBack Kids, customizable lost-and-found QR stickers for children's belongings. Please confirm whether you can supply **one fully personalized ISO A4 (210 × 297 mm) backing sheet per order**, with **six individual kiss-cut stickers in a 2 × 3 layout**. The A4 release liner must stay completely intact and flat; **no through-cutting or trimming**.
>
> Rounded design size 60 × 31 mm; optional contour/silhouette design 55 × 58 mm (contour still to be approved). We can provide vector QR codes, artwork, customer-selected names, and separated vector cut guides; each sheet has **different text and six distinct QR codes**, and **every customer order has different content**.
>
> Preferred medium: white vinyl / other water-resistant synthetic adhesive, matte or other QR-friendly protective laminate. Please advise printer-required safe zones, cut line / spot-colour setup, colour profile, artwork resolution, bleed, QR minimum module size and physical QR scan tests.
>
> Can you accept a **single-sheet development proof** and then process **individually different A4 sheets in small batches**? Please state minimum quantity *per design*, proof charge, price per distinct personalized A4 sheet (including material/cutting/tax), expected production lead time, and any capacity constraints. We need to retain the full A4 liner for rigid flat-mailer packaging.
>
> This is **a capabilities/quotation enquiry only**; we are **not placing an order now**. Please do not request real parent/child names, live QR IDs, private shipping addresses or payments at this stage.

## Acceptance gates before any paid printing
1. Written acceptance of single unique personalized A4 sheets per customer, or a viable batching strategy for distinct sheets.
2. Written confirmation of kiss-cut that preserves all A4 release liner and receives 6 independent cut contours.
3. Material+laminate specification and trial QR scan on actual laminated label after handling.
4. Physical quality proof and 300 DPI art-master checks; avoid printing low-resolution preview art.
5. Cut-guide and print-file specification must be reconciled with `printing/A4_SIX_PACK_PRINT_AND_PACKAGING_SPEC.md`.
6. Total sheet cost must leave adequate contribution after Shiprocket postage, mailer, payment fees, taxes, returns and marketing. Prices ₹249/₹449/₹699 are **planned pack sales prices**, not confirmed profit.
7. Customer privacy, data processing restrictions, deletion policy, and no reuse of parent/child QR artwork.
8. Owner approval before contacting on behalf, sharing live records, ordering samples, paying any fee or committing vendor.
