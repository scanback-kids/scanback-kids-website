Only 20 approved source template contours are available, so shapes are reused. Different illustration crops and original artwork-specific styling may differ. Visually reviewing contact sheets is not a substitute for per-design visual verification against original approved masters. The 60×31 mm and 55×58 mm production layouts are not yet proven. QR URLs are synthetic; no Supabase allocation has been connected. Independent ink collision/safe-box tests remain required.
