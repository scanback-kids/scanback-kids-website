Designed to help lost things find their way home

The full lost-and-found service activates when a purchased sticker is registered and enabled.

1

Choose and personalize

Choose an illustration and add your child's display name and an optional label such as “Water Bottle”.

2

Attach the registered QR sticker

Each issued sticker links to an individual item record. The parent manages its activation and reports.

3

A finder scans to help

A finder can send a message, optionally share a location, and help the parent arrange a return through the service.
