Privacy Policy

ScanBack Kids provides personalized QR lost-and-found stickers to help return children's belongings. This notice explains the information used by the website, parent dashboard and QR finder service.

Last updated: 17 October 2026. New paid orders are not currently open; the shop shows design previews and existing test records.

1. What information may be used?

  • Parent account and sticker information: account email, login-related data, a child's display name or nickname, item label, QR identifier and activation status.
  • Finder reports: an optional finder name, message, time of report and the scanned sticker reference.
  • Optional finder location: precise coordinates and available accuracy information only if a finder selects the location-sharing option and their browser grants permission. A report can be sent without sharing location.
  • Optional finder contact: name and phone number or email only if the finder chooses to provide contact details after reporting an item and agrees to share them with the registered parent.
  • Test orders and support: order and design selections; for internal test purchases, the information entered for checkout or delivery; and messages sent to support@scanback.in.
  • Technical and security information: data needed for account sessions, QR functionality, error handling, website operation and CAPTCHA abuse protection.

2. Why is information used?

Information supports parent accounts and their QR stickers, finder reports and notifications, optional return arrangements, customer support, test-order management and service security. Please use a child's nickname or limited display name where possible, and do not enter a home address or other sensitive details as a sticker label.

3. What does a person scanning a QR code see?

Someone with a working QR link for an active sticker can view its registered child's display name and item name to help identify it. The public finder page is designed not to display the parent's private email address or phone number. Deactivated or unavailable stickers do not show the normal reporting form.

Because physical QR codes can be scanned by other people, parents should avoid adding a full legal name or other identifying details unnecessarily.

4. Sharing reports, location and contact details

Finder reports are provided to the registered parent to help return the item, including an optional message, name and location if actively shared. Optional finder contact details are shared privately with the parent for coordinating return, rather than displayed on the public sticker page. Do not use the finder form for emergencies.

5. Technology and service providers

Our website and features use third-party infrastructure to operate. This includes hosting and databases (such as Vercel and Supabase), notification delivery (such as Resend), CAPTCHA protection (Cloudflare Turnstile) and customer support email (Titan). Test checkout functionality also uses Razorpay in test mode; public paid checkout is not open. These providers may process relevant technical or transaction information when their features are used.

Browser storage may be used to keep a signed-in session or the state of a finder report. Hosting and security systems may also process technical information such as IP addresses. We do not ask finders to share GPS location by default.

6. Security and access

Parent account information and scan history are intended for the appropriate account holder, and QR administration uses access checks. No internet service can promise perfect security; use a strong unique password and deactivate QR stickers that should no longer be usable.

7. Retention and privacy requests

Different account, support, scan and test-order records may need to be kept for different operational, technical or legal reasons. A specific retention schedule has not yet been finalized for publication. If you want to ask what information is held about you, request a correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern, please contact us. Some deletions may affect active QR services or records required to be retained.

✉️ Contact privacy support

8. Parents and children

The service is intended to be set up and managed by parents or guardians, not by children independently. Parents should choose limited display names for children's stickers and review the information that becomes visible when a QR link is scanned. For concerns about a child's information, email support@scanback.in.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service, data practices or legal requirements change. The last-updated date above will identify the current version. Contact us at support@scanback.in or visit our Contact Us page.
